📖 Zakat Fundamental Principles

1. Nisab (The Threshold)

The minimum net wealth a Muslim must hold before Zakat becomes obligatory. For cash and gold, it corresponds to the current market value of 85 grams of pure gold.

2. Haul (The Holding Period)

The wealth must stay equal to or above the Nisab for one full Islamic lunar year (approx. 354 days) without falling below the threshold.

3. The 2.5% Rate (1/40th)

Eligible wealth (savings, investment gold, commercial trade inventory) is zakated at 2.5%. Formula: Zakatable Wealth × 0.025.

4. The 8 Asnaf Groups (Surah At-Tawbah 9:60)

Zakat is strictly distributed only to the 8 divine categories:

• Fakir: Destitute without sustenance
• Miskin: Needy with partial income
• Amil: Appointed Zakat workers
• Muallaf: Reconciled hearts / converts
• Riqab: Emancipating captives/servants
• Gharimin: Permissible debt burden
• Fi Sabilillah: Cause of Allah
• Ibnus Sabil: Stranded traveler
